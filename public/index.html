Understanding Wholesale Mandi Benchmarks vs. Retail Margins

Wholesale / Mandi Rate: The auction clearing price at primary APMC yards (e.g. Bowenpally, Enumamula). These are bulk prices traded between commission agents and wholesale traders.

Retail Price Range: The end-consumer price at Rythu Bazars, local vegetable stalls, and retail stores. The 25%–50% margin covers grading loss, spoilage, last-mile freight, and vendor retail markup.

Net Farmer Realization: When selling at APMCs, farmers incur loading, transport (₹1.5–₹3/kg), and mandi deductions. By selling directly on KisanSaathi, farmers eliminate commission middlemen and can capture rates 10%–18% above wholesale mandi benchmarks while offering buyers lower-than-retail procurement prices.

Diagnostic Fix: Incoming SMS arrives in Vivo Inbox but missing in TextBee Activity?

On Vivo Android phones (Funtouch OS), background power management blocks third-party SMS listeners unless explicitly exempted:

1. Settings > Battery > Background power consumption: Set TextBee to "High background power usage".
2. Settings > Applications & Permissions > Autostart: Toggle TextBee to Enabled.
3. App Permissions > SMS: Set TextBee to "Allow all the time".
4. Recent Apps: Swipe down on TextBee card and tap the 🔒 padlock icon to prevent process termination.
5. Notification Shade: Verify the continuous notification "TextBee Gateway is active" is showing.
